# One-minute demo script

**0:00–0:08 — Problem**  
“Selo Vivo lets a Brazilian cooperative prove a sustainability credential is valid without sharing its identity, exact location, or audit documents.”

**0:08–0:18 — Wallet and network**  
Show Preview/Preprod selection. Connect 1AM. Point out the shortened address and explain that disconnect clears the in-memory session.

**0:18–0:29 — Gemini composer**  
Edit the public buyer requirement and click **Compositor Gemini**. Show the minimal plan and state that Gemini sees only public text and harmless labels—never the credential.

**0:29–0:42 — Privacy review**  
Show the local credential, masked ID, disclosure cards, and privacy boundary. Say: “Class, validity result, request tag, and aggregate count are public; identity, exact expiry, biome evidence, and holder secret remain private witnesses.”

**0:42–0:55 — Midnight proof**  
Click **Gerar prova privada**, approve the 1AM prompts, and show the finalized Preprod contract and proof transaction IDs.

**0:55–1:00 — Verification**  
Show the public counter and finish: “The buyer gets a verifiable answer, while the producer keeps the credential.”

## Recording checklist

- Use Chrome at 1440×900 or 1920×1080.
- Hide bookmarks and unrelated extensions.
- Pre-fund 1AM with Preprod DUST.
- Start FastAPI with Neon and `GEMINI_API_KEY` configured.
- Keep the final receipt visible for at least three seconds.
- Export MP4 at 1080p, 30 fps, under 60 seconds.
